In Renu's own words:

For the past 20+ years, Newark has been home to my family. As a mother of two daughters growing up in our community, I have experienced firsthand the hopes every parent has for their children - to receive an excellent education, discover their strengths, and graduate for a bright future.

Education transformed my own life. After earning a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, and an MBA from UC Berkeley, I built a career spanning over three decades leading innovation - from helping shape mobile technologies to currently advancing the safe and responsible use of AI. Along the way, I have learned - great organizations succeed through vision, fiscal responsibility, and continuous improvement.

As an eight-armed juggler, I also lead a Girl Scouts troop, direct a Tri-City foreign language program for K-12 students, and serve on the boards of Alameda County Science and Engineering Fair (ACSEF) and American Society of Engineers of Indian Origin (ASEI) - all with an objective of making our communities stronger!

I will bring community-focused leadership, responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars, a commitment to stronger academics, STEM, and the whole-child approach - ensuring every Newark student has the opportunity to thrive.

My intent is to listen, lead with integrity, and work to enable our students for a lifetime of success.

a little about me

A technologist who never left the classroom

Two decades in Newark, a career built on solving hard problems, and a life spent showing up for kids.

Renu has spent nearly three decades helping shape the tech industry, building products and leading teams at some of the biggest names in software — from Sun Microsystems and Oracle to IBM and Broadcom — and today leads AI product strategy for the enterprise. That same discipline in strategic planning, team leadership, and operational excellence is what she brings to community service.

She is a recognized industry speaker and advisory board member, invited to share her perspective on responsible AI adoption at conferences and institutions worldwide — but her most fulfilling work has always been closer to home: a Newark resident of 20 years, raising her own children here, and working to make our world a better place every day.
